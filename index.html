Grace’s Trail, hikes drawn as maps

Grace’s TrailAbout meContact us

Grace’s Trail is a growing atlas of hikes, each one drawn as a 3D map from real elevation data: the climbs, the creek crossings, the overlooks, and the numbered stops worth pausing at, with the animals and flowers you might meet along the way.

It started in the San Gabriel Valley and now reaches across the country: the Malibu coast and Mount Baldy, Zion and the Grand Canyon, Glacier and the Tetons, the Smokies, Acadia, even Kauaʻi and Alaska. Grace is already looking up at where to go next. Zoom the map, pick a trail, and go explore.
